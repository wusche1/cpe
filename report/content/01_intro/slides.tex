\begin{frame}{Causal Perturbative Elicitation}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item Unsupervised: discovers rank-1 LoRA factors that surface latent behaviours.
    \item Data-efficient: a population of directions from as little as one example.
    \item Already shown to elicit personas, unlock password-locked skills, and reason near matched-compute GRPO.
  \end{itemize}
